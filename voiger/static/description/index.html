Voiger for Odoo

Make and take Voiger calls without leaving Odoo

What it does

  • Adds a phone button to the Odoo top bar.
  • Opens the Voiger dialer in a panel on every Odoo screen.
  • Keeps a call running while you move between Odoo apps.
  • Hide and show the panel without ending the call.

What you need

  • A Voiger account. The app is free to install; calling needs a paid Voiger agent login from voiger.io.
  • Google Chrome, with microphone access allowed for your Odoo address.

This app connects to Voiger's service at app.voiger.io. Calls are handled by Voiger, not by Odoo.

Getting started

  1. Install the app.
  2. Click the phone button in the top bar.
  3. Sign in with your Voiger account.
  4. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks, then dial.
