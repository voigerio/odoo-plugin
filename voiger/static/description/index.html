Voiger for Odoo

Make and take Voiger calls without leaving Odoo

What it does

  • Adds a phone button to the Odoo top bar.
  • Opens the Voiger dialer in a panel on every Odoo screen.
  • Keeps a call running while you move between Odoo apps.
  • Hide and show the panel without ending the call.
  • Click any phone number in Odoo to call it with Voiger.
  • Incoming calls open the dialer and the caller's contact.
  • Each call is logged as a note on the contact, lead or opportunity, as answered, not answered or missed.
  • Create a contact for an unknown number in one click.
  • The note shows the result, talk and hold time, queue and agent, your Voiger notes, the AI summary and the recording.
  • Voiger tags are added to the contact.
  • Pin the panel to keep it open while you work in Odoo.

What you need

  • A Voiger account. The app is free to install; calling needs a paid Voiger agent login from voiger.io.
  • Google Chrome, with microphone access allowed for your Odoo address.

This app connects to Voiger's service at app.voiger.io. Calls are handled by Voiger, not by Odoo. The app sends Voiger the numbers you call.

Getting started

  1. Install the app.
  2. Click the phone button in the top bar.
  3. Sign in with your Voiger account.
  4. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks, then dial.
